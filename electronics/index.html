PRACTICE OVERVIEW

Building Leadership for the Companies Designing, Manufacturing and Scaling the Technologies of Tomorrow

The electronics, semiconductor and industrial technology sectors are undergoing unprecedented transformation. Artificial Intelligence, Industry 4.0, semiconductor manufacturing, Electronics System Design & Manufacturing (ESDM), robotics, automation, smart factories, digital engineering and global supply chain realignment are redefining the future of manufacturing and technology.

As India emerges as one of the world's fastest-growing destinations for electronics manufacturing, semiconductor investment and advanced engineering, organisations require leaders capable of building world-class manufacturing operations, engineering capabilities, R&D, product innovation and globally competitive businesses.

At CSuite Talent Management Consulting, we partner with electronics manufacturers, semiconductor companies, engineering organisations, industrial technology enterprises, OEMs, EMS providers, ESDM organisations, investors and multinational corporations to identify, assess and engage exceptional Board, CXO and senior leadership talent.

WHY WE ARE DIFFERENT

We Understand the Electronics Industry from the Inside

Our Founder, Anil Mahajane (MBA – Indian Institute of Foreign Trade), began placing senior professionals in India's consumer electronics industry during its formative years.

Between 1994 and 2003, he supported leadership hiring for Samsung India and LG Electronics India — two organisations that played a significant role in establishing India's modern consumer electronics manufacturing ecosystem. He subsequently expanded his executive search practice across the Middle East, partnering with global manufacturing and technology organisations on senior leadership assignments.
