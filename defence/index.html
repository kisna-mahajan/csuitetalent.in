COMPLIANCE

Our Commitment to Ethics, Confidentiality & Compliance

Every executive search assignment is conducted with professionalism, integrity and respect for applicable laws, regulations, organisational policies and contractual obligations.

CSuite Talent Management Consulting does not:

  • solicit, request or facilitate access to classified, confidential or restricted information
  • encourage any individual to breach employment agreements, confidentiality obligations or security requirements
  • represent itself as acting on behalf of any government department, armed force, defence establishment or public authority
  • undertake executive search activities inconsistent with applicable laws, regulations or organisational policies

Where applicable, organisations and professionals remain responsible for complying with security clearance requirements, post-employment restrictions, confidentiality obligations and all relevant contractual or statutory requirements.
