Client Recognition

Recognition That Reflects Trust, Performance and Enduring Partnerships

The most meaningful recognition does not come from trophies or public accolades. It comes from the confidence clients place in us to deliver leadership that shapes the future of their organisations.

Organisations entrust us with their most consequential leadership decisions — confidential succession planning, cross-border executive hiring, organisational transformation. The recognition we have received reflects that trust, and our commitment to professionalism, integrity and long-term partnerships.

Anil Mahajane, MBA (Indian Institute of Foreign Trade), receiving client recognition from Mr. Arun Kumar Dixit, Projects Director & Technical Development Advisor, and Mr. Salah M. Hakim, Head of Human Resource Management, at the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, Mumbai, following the successful completion of an international executive search assignment for one of Saudi Arabia's leading publicly listed cement companies.

The Assignment

A Proven International Executive Search Programme

In 2010, Anil Mahajane successfully executed a major international executive search programme for one of Saudi Arabia's leading publicly listed cement companies — one of the defining milestones in our journey.

The assignment required identifying, assessing and engaging senior leadership talent across multiple international markets, including executives from Lafarge, Holcim and other leading global manufacturers. Delivery demanded extensive market intelligence, confidential executive engagement and rigorous leadership assessment across borders.

Following successful delivery, Anil Mahajane was formally recognised by the client's senior leadership at a private dinner hosted at the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, Mumbai — acknowledging the identification and engagement of world-class executive talent for one of the Middle East's most respected industrial organisations.

This recognition represents far more than a professional milestone. It reflects the confidence clients place in our ability to manage complex, confidential and strategically important executive search assignments across international markets.
