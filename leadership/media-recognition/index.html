Why This Archive Matters

A Historical Record of India's Transformation

These interviews collectively document a pivotal period in India's economic and corporate evolution — captured in real time across leading national and international publications.

  • The early emergence of reverse migration into India
  • The evolution of India's technology and outsourcing industries
  • The rise of India as a global employment destination
  • The growing internationalisation of Indian companies
  • The changing dynamics of executive search and leadership hiring

Many of the ideas articulated in these interviews became mainstream only years later. Viewed in hindsight, this archive captures a remarkably early vision of the future of work, recruitment and India's place in the global talent economy.
