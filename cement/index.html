Practice Overview

Leadership Is the Decisive Competitive Advantage

The cement and building materials industry is undergoing one of its most significant transformations in decades. Capacity expansion, sustainable manufacturing, green cement technologies, alternative fuels, digital manufacturing, ESG commitments, carbon reduction, infrastructure investment and international expansion are redefining what leadership looks like.

The challenge is no longer finding experienced executives. The challenge is identifying leaders capable of improving operational performance, executing large capital projects, strengthening sustainability initiatives and creating long-term shareholder value.

At CSuite Talent Management Consulting, we partner with cement manufacturers, building materials companies, mining organisations, EPC contractors and industrial enterprises to identify, assess and engage exceptional Board, CXO and senior leadership talent. With more than 30 years of executive search experience, we help organisations build leadership teams that deliver operational excellence, sustainable growth and enduring business value.

We don’t simply match resumes. We assess leadership.

International Track Record

A Proven International Cement Executive Search Legacy

Our experience in the cement industry extends well beyond traditional executive recruitment. One of the defining milestones in our journey was successfully executing international executive search assignments for one of the largest cement manufacturers in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Anil Mahajane received a prestigious client appreciation award in 2010 for successfully delivering senior leadership talent from multiple international markets - including executive leaders from Europe, Canada, Egypt, South Africa, Indonesia, Malaysia, Sri Lanka and India, and senior executives from Lafarge and Holcim.

This achievement demonstrated our ability to execute highly confidential, cross-border executive search assignments while maintaining exceptional standards of leadership assessment, quality and professionalism.
